What has changed in Circa, and in the history behind it. Newest first.

September 2026 Latest

Fewer repeats, and you can now filter your timeline

  • Chips above each timeline let you narrow it by how far an event reached and by what kind of event it was.
  • A few hundred duplicate entries were merged in the history itself, keeping the better title and description of each pair. More will follow.
  • World-reaching moments such as the moon landing now always appear, instead of being crowded out of a busy timeline.
  • Long events no longer show a stray extra card near the start of a life when they span several decades.

Spotted something that looks wrong, or an event that should be here and is not? The suggestion link at the foot of any timeline is the best way to reach us, and the thumbs on each card feed straight into the scoring.
