September 2026 Latest

A bigger, better-curated history, and filters for your timeline

  • Circa now runs on version 0.6 of the GeoHistory dataset: larger, more carefully curated, with a few hundred duplicate entries merged so the same event is less likely to reach you twice under two different names. It also adds a tier of world events — moments that reached everyone alive, such as the moon landing — which now always appear rather than being crowded out of a busy timeline.
  • Filters above each timeline let you narrow it by how far an event reached and by what kind of event it was. Local on its own is worth a look.
  • Long events no longer show a stray extra card near the start of a life when they span several decades.

Spotted something that looks wrong, or an event that should be here and is not? The suggestion link at the foot of any timeline is the best way to reach us, and the thumbs on each card feed straight into the scoring.
