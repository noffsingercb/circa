Why this exists

Many years ago, I spent a lot of time searching genealogy records for my family, and loved finding out when and where my ancestors lived. Because of my love of history, the thought of placing those ancestors in history excited me.

Sites like Ancestry.com give you a timeline of a person's life, laying in their relatives and the key events of that life — but they were very sparse on the real historical events that place a person in the real world.

I had the idea to use publicly available history data to create a geolocated and scored database of significant events — what became GeoHistory — so that an app could build a timeline for any set of life events entered into it. Using Notion AI, I was able to create Circa with that in mind: take any life events, and find the history that happened around them.

I hope you enjoy this tool as much as I do, and stay tuned for further updates and improvements to the GeoHistory database and this app.
